About pyMorseRunner

pyMorseRunner attempts to be a faithful port of Morse Runner 1.68, created by Alex Shovkoplyas, VE3NEA (Copyright © 2004–2006, Afreet Software, Inc. • dxatlas.com/MorseRunner).

For over two decades, Alex’s elegant baseband I/Q DSP filter design, windowed log-magnitude AGC, Rayleigh fading, and dynamic-programming pileup caller AI have been the gold standard for CW operators preparing for CQ WW, WPX, IARU HST, CWops CWT, and the K1USN Slow Speed Test. We gratefully acknowledge VE3NEA’s generosity in releasing Morse Runner as open-source software for the global amateur radio community.
